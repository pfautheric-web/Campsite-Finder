🎫 New to booking campsites? The cheat sheet ▼

When reservations open

  • Recreation.gov (national forests, Acadia, Army Corps) — rolling 6 months ahead, released daily at 10am ET
  • MA, NH, CT, PA & NY state parks — rolling ~6 months ahead on ReserveAmerica
  • Vermont — rolling 11 months ahead, daily at 9am
  • Maine — whole season opens Feb 1; book winter for summer
  • Baxter (Katahdin) — opens in stages starting mid-January; summer weekends go in minutes

The last-minute playbook

  • Acadia backdoor: 10% of sites are held back until 14 days before arrival, released 10am ET — set a phone reminder
  • Cancellation churn peaks Sunday–Monday evenings as people bail on next weekend — re-run the live check then
  • Go Sun–Thu: weekend-only scarcity is the real problem; midweek almost always has space
  • Hipcamp (private land) rarely sells out — the reliable fallback when parks are full

Beginner tips

  • "FCFS" = first-come, first-served: no reservation possible — show up before ~noon Friday
  • "Walk-in" site = park, then carry gear a short distance; quieter and easier to snag
  • Sites labeled tent-only face less competition than ones that also fit RVs
  • A vault toilet is an outhouse; "no hookups" only matters for RVs
  • Most parks hold your site until checkout time the next day — arriving late Friday night is fine

How this works: Federal campgrounds (national forests, Acadia, Army Corps) are checked live against Recreation.gov's availability API — the count shown is tent/standard sites open for every night of your stay. State-park systems don't offer public APIs, so those rows link directly to that specific park's booking page (with your arrival date pre-filled where the system supports it). Maine and New Jersey run single statewide booking portals with no per-park links — pick the park once you're inside. Drive times are estimates from straight-line distance (×1.25 road factor at ~55 mph) — good for sorting, not navigation. Live checks probe four network routes (direct + three CORS proxies), lock onto whichever works in your browser, and pressing the button again retries only the failures.
